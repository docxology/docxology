105 MIN / FIRST PASS

From probability to Active Inference

Start with a small update rule, then distinguish that teaching example from broader accounts of cognition and Bayesian mechanics.

Prerequisites: Basic fractions; familiarity with conditional probability helps with the final paper.

  1. demo / 10 min

    Binary Bayesian update

    Change a prior and likelihoods. Explain why an observation changes the posterior and identify impossible evidence.

  2. work / 25 min

    Of woodlice and men: A Bayesian account of cognition, life and consciousness. An interview with Karl Friston.

    Read the interview for conceptual context. Separate discussion of the Free Energy Principle from experimental evidence.

  3. video / 30 min

    Fundamentals of Active Inference (Onboarding & Introduction, Session 1) April 21, 2026

    Sample the Fundamentals onboarding discussion and note unfamiliar terms to revisit in the monograph.

  4. work / 40 min

    A Worked Example of the Bayesian Mechanics of Classical Objects

    Inspect the worked example's assumptions and equations; identify the extra structure beyond the binary update.

120 MIN / FIRST PASS

Ants, reinforcement and collective behavior

Compare an abstract reinforcement process with empirical measurements and specific models of ant behavior.

Prerequisites: Introductory biology and probability; no programming required for the first three steps.

  1. demo / 10 min

    Reinforced urn

    Repeat a seed, vary reinforcement, and explain which biological mechanisms the urn leaves out.

  2. work / 30 min

    Gene expression variation in the brains of harvester ant foragers is associated with collective behavior

    Identify the measured variables and distinguish reported associations from causal mechanisms.

  3. work / 35 min

    A single-pheromone model accounts for empirical patterns of ant colony foraging previously modeled using two pheromones

    Compare a specific pheromone model's assumptions and outputs with the abstract urn.

  4. video / 25 min

    ActInf Livestream #029.0 ~ “Active Inferants: An Active Inference Framework for Ant Colony Behavior”

    Sample the Active Inferants discussion and list the modeling choices that need their own empirical validation.

  5. software / 20 min

    MetaInformAnt

    Inspect the repository's data workflow and evidence boundaries before considering a local run.

120 MIN / FIRST PASS

Information commons and cognitive security

Move from narrative ecosystems to standards and operational agent skills, keeping conceptual and implementation claims distinct.

Prerequisites: Interest in information systems, governance or security; basic repository navigation for the final step.

  1. work / 30 min

    Narrative Information Ecosystems: Conflict and Trust on the Endless Frontier

    Identify the framework's units of analysis and its account of conflict and trust.

  2. work / 30 min

    Structuring the Information Commons: Open Standards and Cognitive Security

    Compare the role of open standards with the preceding narrative perspective.

  3. work / 35 min

    The Properties, Processes, and Perspectives Inter-Framework (P3IF): Multiplexing interdisciplinary requirements frameworks to manage information risk and foster cognitive security

    Follow the distinctions among properties, processes and perspectives in the proposed framework.

  4. work / 25 min

    CogSecSkills: Multiharness Agentic Skills for Cognitive Security

    Inspect the skill architecture and follow its associated software link; distinguish packaged guidance from demonstrated security outcomes.
