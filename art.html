Art Gallery

A collection of 943 pen and ink drawings exploring the intersection of biology, cognitive science, philosophy, and complex systems.

High-investment drawings (10–20 hrs each) · Pen & ink on paper · Published in Taschen's "On NFT" (2024)

Curio Cards NFTs Curio Cards Monograph Art & Synergetics Domain Full Flickr Archive Christie's Auction Instagram ← Back to Main Site

What this gallery is. A searchable index of the pen-and-ink drawings — the visual wing of the research practice, spanning ants, Active Inference, Synergetics, and American subjects. Every drawing carries a title, a creation date, tags, and a Flickr source record; the complete structured export is data/artworks.json, with a compact index at data/artworks-index.json. Every tile opens the drawing's own page under artworks/, and the themed hubs — Neuroscience & Molecular Biology, Collective Behavior & Ants, Sacred Geometry & Kabbalistic Triangles, Active Inference, and Americana & Seals — group the archive for browsing and search engines alike.

How to use it. The search box filters by title, tag, or description; the sort menu orders by date, title, or Flickr views; the size buttons change tile density. Each tile links to the drawing's own static page — with the description, tags, license, related works, and a link to the full-resolution file on Flickr — and opens the lightbox in JavaScript-capable browsers as a progressive enhancement. Without JavaScript, the page still serves the newest thirty tiles directly in the HTML, and the complete plain-HTML artwork index reaches every drawing — nothing here requires a client-side render to read.

Context. The gallery also records the early-Ethereum-art thread: drawings such as "CurioDAO" and "Distributed Development (Ethereum's Timecircle)" (2017) sit alongside the Curio Cards work — pen-and-ink cards issued as early Ethereum art NFTs in 2017 and later offered in a Christie's auction — with the full history in the monograph.
