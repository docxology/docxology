# Daniel Friedman's Curio Cards

These are the complete, unmodified card images served by the official Curio
Cards website, displayed on the artist's homepage:

| File | Card | Official page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `24-complexity.jpg` | 24 — Complexity | [Complexity](https://curio.cards/card/24/) |
| `25-passion.jpg` | 25 — Passion | [Passion](https://curio.cards/card/25/) |
| `26-education.jpg` | 26 — Education | [Education](https://curio.cards/card/26/) |

The artwork is by Daniel Friedman. The original drawing and the framed Curio
Card are different images; the homepage intentionally displays the associated
card rather than a substitute drawing or decorative background.

[provenance.json](provenance.json) records the exact source image URL, fetch
date, SHA-256, byte length, and 900 × 1200 dimensions for each JPEG. No cropping,
recoloring, or recompression has been applied.

The official [artwork licensing policy](https://docs.curio.cards/info/artwork-licensing)
permits display in galleries and documentation, while each artist retains the
rights to their artwork. For Daniel Friedman's Cards 24–26, the policy states
CC BY for holders and CC BY-NC for non-holders, as of 2022; it does not specify a
Creative Commons version. The site's general software license does not replace
these artwork terms. This asset bundle supports display and is not a claim of
permission to create or sell derivatives.
