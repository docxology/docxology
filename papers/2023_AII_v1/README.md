## Key Findings

- The Institute traces its origin to a 2020 co-founder collaboration, the ActInfLab formed in 2021, and its 2022 transition into a Delaware non-profit.
- The document reports that in 2022 there were 103 PubMed citations for "Active Inference" out of 1,772,674 publications.
- The authors state the Institute has hosted or facilitated hundreds of open-source licensed products serving Awareness, Education, Commons, Support and Governance.
- The authors identify key challenges including onboarding, tool usability, scalability of support functions, communications, and administrative and financial support.
- The authors describe choosing an 'open' ecosystem approach over a closed, laboratory-centric one that might have siloed Active Inference academically.
