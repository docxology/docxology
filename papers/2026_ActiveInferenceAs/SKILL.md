## Methods

Primary methods and techniques applied in this work:

- **Executable GNN specifications and exact categorical inference** — Imported model specifications are evaluated by an exact categorical runtime.
- **Separate training and synthetic evaluation** — Likelihood fitting uses labeled training data separate from evaluation in the specified sensor task.
- **Paired simulation comparisons** — Independent simulation replicates retain predictive scores, observation costs and uncertainty.

## Key Findings

Core contributions and results:

- Reliable metadata and further sensing improve classification within the specified synthetic sensor task.
- Misleading metadata and world-model mismatch can leave exact inference confidently mistaken.
- Mathematical coherence and optimization under preferences do not establish representation adequacy or justify those preferences.
- The demonstrations establish conditional computational consequences and do not validate broader biological or social interpretations.
