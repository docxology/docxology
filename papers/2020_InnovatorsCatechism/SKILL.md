# The Innovator's Catechism

**Richard J. Cordes, Daniel A. Friedman, Steven E. Phelan** (2020) · Cognitive Security

## Context

This work addresses topics in **Cognitive Security**: operations orders, innovation teams, Innovator's Catechism, team alignment.

## Methods

Primary methods and techniques applied in this work:

- **Historical review of military, business and gray-zone operations orders** — Reviews the history, development and organizational impact of military OPORDs and their business counterparts as the basis for the proposed catechism.
- **Adapting the Facilitator's Catechism with Blank's innovation pipeline** — Combines the authors' earlier catechism-styled OPORD with Steve Blank's innovation pipeline to derive stage-specific question sets.
- **Six-stage partition from Ideation to Integration** — Partitions a startup's journey into Ideation, Curation, Pitch, Exploration, Incubation and Integration, each with its own mission and information requirements.
- **Example renderings of the catechism to NABC, canvas and slide formats** — Illustrates reformatting a filled-in catechism into other pitch formats such as canvas variants, the Heilmeier Catechism and NABC.

## Key Findings

Core contributions and results:

- Presents the Innovator's Catechism, a catechism-styled operations order for early-stage, market-facing innovation teams.
- The IC keeps the Facilitator's Catechism sections but replaces Implications of Outcome with Cost and Benefit.
- Unlike other OPORDs, the IC's questions depend on the team's pipeline stage, forming a 'family' of catechisms that grows as the team progresses.
- The authors identify catechism format, narrative development, versioning and modularity as the IC's distinguishing features.
- The authors recommend IC use in hackathons, accelerators and incubators, and describe it as a first attempt at a catechism-styled OPORD for this area.
