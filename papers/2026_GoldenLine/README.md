## Abstract

A directional instrument for recording long-horizon aspirations and observable movement toward them. It returns one of four directional readings per aspiration against a versioned registry and deliberately computes no aggregate: there is no virtue score, and NOT_OBSERVED means no valid entry was admitted rather than that nobody looked.
