Overview

Curated abstract when available.

The selected FEP_Formal 2.1.0 manuscript describes 168 stable Lean topics across five areas, using Lean 4.35.0 and Mathlib 4.35.0. It reports a semantic audit with 149 directly formalized rows at narrowed scopes and separates compilation maturity from scientific adequacy. Native receipt-backed compilation and optional Hermes/OpenGauss execution remain different evidence contracts. The manuscript does not prove the empirical FEP, generic Markov blankets or equivalence of its motivating formalisms; this catalog refresh does not rerun Lean, numerical examples or provider workflows.
