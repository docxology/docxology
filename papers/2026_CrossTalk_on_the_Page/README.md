## Abstract

> A literature review, corpus linguistic analysis, and visual study of CrossTalk, the defense software engineering journal. The retained corpus comprises 281 issue PDFs and 9,660 measured physical pages, with 2,708 distinct complete AI-assisted journal article appraisals and exact article counts for 270 of 281 retained PDF scopes. All 197 surviving physical pages in the 11 open original print scopes were examined. The 66 confirmed complete bodies link to existing AI-assisted appraisals, including three embedded web bodies. The archival comparisons trace alternate text and figures to missing or obscured print regions. The historical totals of original issues and printed articles remain unknown. Exact article counts cover 148 of the 158 priority PDFs. All 53 staged appraisals have supporting source evidence. The study presents newly verified provenance for the March 1992 reengineering method and distinguishes original print gaps from separately circulating articles and figures. The preprint and companion tables provide original analysis and derived measurements. Source locators identify third-party publications available under their own terms. The related software link identifies the tagged analysis source and portable inputs on GitHub.
