Overview

Extracted from the local README when available.

Research groups routinely need to pitch their work — to funders, partners, or collaborators — yet pitch decks are almost never treated as reproducible research artifacts: they are hand-assembled in proprietary slide tools, contain unverifiable claims, and cannot be regenerated when the underlying facts change. template pitch deck closes that gap. It generates six artifacts from one token-resolved content source — short, medium, and long decks, each in both PDF and PPTX — with every numeric claim traced back to a live introspection of the repository it describes, every {{TOKEN}} substitution verified to have actually landed, and every sentence checked against a denylist of pitch-deck clichés.
