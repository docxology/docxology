Abstract
Observer Patch Holography (OPH) proposes that physics is what bounded observers can
establish together: ﬁnite patches compare records on shared boundaries, repair disagreements,
and treat as objective whatever survives every comparison. The universe, on this view, is a
timeless self-referential structure whose observers recover its speciﬁcation from their records
and realize it, so that it reads itself into existence. Nothing lies outside to impose premises,
so the recovered speciﬁcation owes no a-priori justiﬁcation; it has to run and reproduce what
observers measure.
That speciﬁcation is a candidate, an executable observer: a twelve-port carrier, a native
repair law, retained records and record-controlled feedback, joined in one interacting quantum
process of electric ﬂux, charged matter and a scalar ﬁeld whose feedback response is certiﬁed
nonzero in the inﬁnite-occupation limit. Observers whose regions overlap on one carrier share a
single complete law of ﬁelds, local event clocks and feedback, and on a reﬁning spherical screen
one repair history deﬁnes a common continuum operator for Gaussian ﬁelds, their decay and
ﬁnite-speed waves. On a declared radial product over that screen, every ﬁnite menu of observer
readouts admits, to any tolerance, one smooth static (1 + 3) -dimensional Lorentzian eﬀective
ﬁeld theory. Finite theorems turn the speciﬁcation’s readouts into familiar structure: schedule-
independent public records, the Born rule and Tsirelson bound on completed records, a carrier
whose slow response is exactly three-dimensional, the Standard Model gauge Lie algebra with an
anomaly-free generation, a source-derived (1 + 3) causal precursor with proper-time ratios from
record counts, and the Einstein equation from a realized null balance. We state the acceptance
criteria of the construction, which premises it discharges, which it takes as declared inputs, and
the frozen tests that can reject its branches.

1 Introduction
Physics usually begins with a world and asks how observers ﬁt into it. Observer Patch Holography
(OPH) begins with observers and asks what world they can establish together. Its observers are
ﬁnite. Each holds a patch of local state, reads a boundary it shares with its neighbors, keeps durable
records, and repairs its state when records disagree; it accepts only repairs that preserve protected
data. OPH identiﬁes an objective fact with a record that survives every authorized comparison.
Quantum probability, Lorentzian kinematics, gravitational dynamics and gauge structure enter
standard physics through distinct mathematical inputs [ 17, 18, 19, 53, 60]; OPH asks whether
they are diﬀerent public readouts of one ﬁnite observer architecture. Space, time, matter and
classical objects are eﬀective descriptions of what observers can measure and jointly interpret; no
observer-independent continuum is assumed beneath them.
The proposal about the whole is closure. The universe is a timeless, self-referential structure
whose observers recover the speciﬁcation of their own world from completed records and build
hardware whose execution realizes that same history, including its builders. The structure reads
itself into existence. This changes what a premise has to do. A theory set against an external
world must justify its postulates in advance or by ﬁt; a structure with no outside has no place from
which a postulate could be imposed. Whatever observers recover and implement therefore needs no
further justiﬁcation: the carrier, the observer algebra, the repair law and two closure coordinates
are justiﬁed by being recovered, by running, and by reproducing what observers measure. The
same rule cuts the other way. Nothing may be assumed that observers have not recovered, and
every change of description they can make must be implemented by the structure’s own operations.
Agreement ﬁxes what counts as shared, so “our universe” is the world described by the public

2 The observer
2.1 Patches, records and repair
Deﬁnition 2.1 (Observer patch). An observer patch is a ﬁnite object with local state, an observable
boundary map, durable records, readback, and a set of authorized repair moves. A patch sees a
fragment of the world: its own state and the boundary data of its authorized overlaps.
Deﬁnition 2.2 (Overlap and repair) . For patches xi, xj with an authorized overlap e = (i, j ), both
induced boundary records must agree for the pair to hold public data. A repair move changes local
state while preserving protected readout. A conﬁguration is a normal form when no authorized
repair applies.
An observer in this sense is deﬁned by its access and record interfaces. It reads, writes and
retains records, and it uses retained records to choose its next operation. Nothing in the deﬁnition
refers to a background space, a global clock or a pre-assigned list of particles. Those enter later as
readouts that observers can share.

6.4 An entry point without a starting point
The exposition of this paper enters the loop at the executable speciﬁcation. That choice reﬂects

No temporal paradox arises. The closure equation relates parts of one timeless object, with no
global time coordinate along which information travels backward. Observers recover the speciﬁ-

point. Repair descent is a well-founded order on authorized updates used to prove termination and

What is found and what is made become two roles in one structure. A rule invented for a game
has consequences its inventor discovers. Here the architecture that observers discover constrains
what they can build, and what they build belongs to the structure from which the discovery is made.
The same reading answers the question why anything exists at the level the hypothesis can answer
it: the question includes its questioner, and a complete inhabited structure whose dependencies
constitute its operation needs no further act of actualization [ 99]. A demand for something in
addition to the complete relational structure is, on this reading, misplaced. If several closures
qualify, the hypothesis admits several worlds, and self-consistency alone has not selected ours.

9 Discussion and comparison
Operational reconstructions derive quantum structure from information and composition principles
[17, 18, 19]. OPH makes a narrower statement at the quantum stage: it takes the ﬁnite algebra-
state representation as an input and contributes the completed-record surface on which the event,
conditioning and correlation identities operate, together with the consensus theorem that deﬁnes
completion. Quantum Darwinism locates classical objectivity in redundant environmental records
[44], and the completed-consensus record plays the same public role with redundancy replaced by
protected overlap agreement and an explicit repair dynamics. The cellular-automaton programme
shares the ﬁnite substrate [ 45], while OPH locates the quantum identities on the completed record
surface. Wigner’s-friend extensions constrain agents reasoning about one another’s unﬁnished mea-
surements [46], whose premises concern pre-consensus perspectives that the completed surface does
not represent. Relational quantum mechanics treats physical facts as relational [ 42] and the thermal-

The gravitational branch belongs to the thermodynamic and entanglement-based routes to

The closure hypothesis has precedents of a diﬀerent kind. Wheeler pictured a participatory uni-

might subsume itself [ 103]. OPH adds two demands: the self-subsumption must be inhabited, car-
ried out through records and agents inside the structure, and it must be testable in part, through
an executable speciﬁcation whose observers can be run and whose instruments make predictions.
Lawvere’s ﬁxed-point theorem shows why unrestricted self-representation fails [ 104]; the closure
condition is restricted to the observable and interventional structure that observers can recover.
A useful comparison of uniﬁcation proposals asks how much physical structure follows from
each declared input and which observations distinguish the resulting models; OPH oﬀers a concrete
case with proofs and executable checks attached to individual implications. Formal veriﬁcation mea-
sures deductive reliability, predictive success requires physical realization and eligible measurements,
and neither theorem counts nor numerical coincidences deﬁne a probability that an architecture
describes nature.
10 Conclusion
OPH proposes that physics is what bounded observers can establish together, and that the universe
is the structure in which such observers recover their own speciﬁcation and realize it. Because
nothing lies outside that structure, the speciﬁcation owes no a-priori justiﬁcation; it has to run and
return the world it was recovered from. The speciﬁcation runs. Its observer repairs, records and
acts on its records, its detector draws its coupling from a closure coordinate, and one interacting
quantum process carries ﬂux, charged matter and a scalar ﬁeld through complete measurements
with a certiﬁed feedback response. Overlapping observers on one carrier read a single complete law
of ﬁelds, local clocks and feedback, and joined carriers on a reﬁning screen carry one continuum
operator for Gaussian ﬁelds, their decay and ﬁnite-speed waves. On a declared radial product over
that screen, observers can describe every ﬁnite menu of their readouts, to any tolerance, by one
smooth static (1 + 3)-dimensional Lorentzian ﬁeld theory.
