## Key Findings

- The authors argue that the typology of particular kinds in the target paper can be seen as a possible contribution to mapping the space of possible minds.
- They propose that as particles move from inert to active, conservative, and strange, they can be seen as moving toward higher values on Gray et al.'s 'agency' axis.
- They suggest the typology of particular kinds can serve as an exemplar and underlying scheme for building or revising taxonomies of mind more precisely.
- From their teaching, the authors report finding the target paper's scope and visual representations almost uniquely relevant in educational settings.
