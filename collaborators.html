Collaborators

Key collaborations across Active Inference, Entomology, Cognitive Security, and Synergetics.

Principal Collaborators

The people below anchor the co-author network across the four research lines of this site. Each card names the collaboration's domain, its starting point, and the joint work pages under works/; the full prose record with paper-folder links is COLLABORATORS.md.

KF
Karl Friston
UCL, London
Active Inference, Free Energy Principle

Originator of the free energy principle. Collaboration began with "Of woodlice and men" (2018) and continues through co-authored papers on variational synthesis (2023), evolutionary dynamics, federated inference (2024), and ant cognitive models in Active Inferants (2021).

DG
Deborah Gordon
Stanford University
Entomology, Collective Behavior

PhD advisor. Foundational work on the behavioral, physiological, and transcriptomic bases of collective behavior in harvester ants across the PhD dissertation (2019), dopamine regulation of foraging (2018), ant genetics review (2016), and brain gene expression (2020).

BJ
Brian Johnson
UC Davis
Social Insect Genomics

Co-mentored NSF Postdoctoral Fellowship at UC Davis (2020–2023), focusing on distributed physiology (2020), neurotransmitter natural variation (2020), and honey bee gene expression (2023).

MA
Mahault Albarracin
AII Scientific Advisory Board
Multi-Agent Active Inference

Collaborated on multi-agent Active Inference models, particularly on shared protentions (anticipatory representations) in multi-agent systems and cognitive architectures.

MR
Maxwell Ramstead
AII Scientific Advisory Board
Active Inference, Phenomenology

Collaborated on formal Active Inference architectures and multi-agent systems, including work on shared protentions (2024), federated inference (2024), Active Inferants (2021), and distributed science (2023).

RC
Robert (RJ) Cordes
COGSEC.org
Cognitive Security, Meme Research

Founder of COGSEC.org. Developed cognitive security frameworks including The Facilitator's Catechism (2020), Digital Rhetorical Ecosystem Analysis (2021), P3IF (2023), image meme research (2024), and the Cognitive Integrity Framework (2026).

TP
Thomas Parr
UCL, London
Active Inference, Computational Neuroscience

Co-author of the Active Inference textbook (Parr, Pezzulo & Friston, 2022). Co-authored with Friedman on variational synthesis (2023) and federated inference (2024).

TL
Tim Linksvayer
Arizona State University
Evolutionary Genomics, Social Evolution

Co-mentored the postdoctoral work on evolutionary genomics in social insects, contributing to distributed physiology (2020) and work on chemical and transcriptomic diversity in Blattodea (2024).

ES
Eric Saund
Computational Modeling, Ant Foraging

Developed a parsimonious single-pheromone model that accounts for empirical patterns of ant colony foraging previously explained by two-pheromone models, published as a single-pheromone model (2023).

CF
Chris Fields
AII Scientific Advisory Board
Physics, Information Processing, Boundaries

Collaboration on foundational questions connecting physics, information processing, and Active Inference. Friedman produced the transcript and educational materials for Fields' "Physics as Information Processing" course at the Active Inference Institute, published as a course transcript (2023).

RP
Riddhi Pitliya
Thoughtseeds, Meditative States, Active Inference

Co-developed the Thoughtseeds framework — a hierarchical and agentic model for investigating thought dynamics in meditative states using Active Inference — in Thoughtseeds (2025) and shared protentions (2024).

ESø
Eirik Søvik
Consciousness Studies, Social Insects

Explored whether ant colonies can serve as test cases for scientific theories of consciousness in the ant colony as a test for scientific theories of consciousness (2019).
