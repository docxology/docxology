Overview

Presenter program and book of abstracts for the 6th Applied Active Inference Symposium, November 12–13, 2026 (UTC), prepared by the Active Inference Institute. The approved November 1, 2026 public program contains 57 contributions, 54 supplied abstracts and three explicitly pending abstracts. Presenter affiliations, ORCIDs and backgrounds are included only where supplied in the approved 2026 public sources; missing values and composite presenter credits are preserved without inferred identities. Participant information is limited to the designated public 2026 view, rechecked on November 5, 2026. Creator entries preserve the published presenter and group display credits, with Active Inference Institute first; they are not a verified count of individual people. Cover and page-two artwork are generated conceptual illustrations. Source, rendering methods and versioned release: ActiveInferenceInstitute/Symposium .
