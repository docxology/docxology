GNN-based active inference for Battleship

Daniel Ari Friedman

Zenodo

Read PDF on Zenodo (version 0.5.0) Publication source Paper folder on GitHub Read extracted text

Overview

The selected 0.5.0 Battleship manuscript describes exact categorical Bayesian inference conditional on a reproducibly sampled finite fleet catalog and a one-step preference-and-information objective. It reports a prospectively specified 1280-search follow-up across 256 paired units and five strategies, with strategy comparisons limited to the declared generator and objectives. The text explicitly disclaims an unrestricted Battleship solver, a new inference principle or a validated educational intervention. This catalog refresh does not reproduce its seeded analyses or statistical results.
