Overview

This template exemplar maps gold-refining stages onto a scientific manuscript pipeline and describes deterministic token selection, equation-backed formalisms and a project-local claim assay. Its purity and karat vocabulary summarizes configured local validation states rather than external manuscript quality. The deposited edition reports its own contribution checks; external empirical validation of manuscript-quality metrics remains future work, and this catalog review does not rerun the pipeline.
