The Witness Register: Co-Registration Without Aggregation

Daniel Ari Friedman

Zenodo

Read PDF on Zenodo (version 0.1.1) Publication source Paper folder on GitHub Read extracted text

Overview

The selected Witness Register edition describes a co-registration layer that retains line-report envelopes and authored cross-line relations without ranking or aggregating native statuses. It specifies digest-sealed chained states and a requested non-compensatory posture. The text distinguishes intake shape checks from truth checks, notes that the chain tip needs an external anchor, and denies that its posture supplies a decision or permission. These are manuscript-reported design boundaries, not newly executed software guarantees.
