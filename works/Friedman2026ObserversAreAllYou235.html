Overview

Observer Patch Holography models finite observer patches that retain records, compare overlap readbacks and repair disagreement. The deposited r2054 manuscript reports conditional thermodynamic, finite quantum, gauge-response and field constructions under stated repair-law, clock-and-energy calibration, quantum-algebra and matter premises. Its fine-structure comparisons are diagnostics; one executed interval excludes the measured value. The opening text explicitly states that the finite constructions do not establish physical realization or select a unique universe. This catalog summary does not independently verify the mathematics, software or physical claims.
