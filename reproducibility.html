Reproducibility Ledger

What a third party can check about each of 206 catalogued works without asking me for anything.

What this measures

This ledger scores artifact availability, not correctness. A work can score 6/6 and still be wrong, and an important result published before persistent identifiers existed can score low through no fault of its own. The score answers one narrow question: how much of the record is independently checkable from public artifacts alone. Every value is computed from works.json and software.json by one generator, and the build fails if this page drifts from that computation.
