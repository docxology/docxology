Description

In this drawing, the scientist (at bottom left) is studying a "gene". In their depiction of the "gene", the scientist neglects to represent the fact that the environment is what controls the "gene" like a marionette. Much to the detriment of students and other researchers, the scientist defends their genetic reductionism by claiming: "But look at my drawing! All is see is genes!". Peace be with them, for they err and know not....
