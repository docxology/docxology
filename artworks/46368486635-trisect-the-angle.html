"Qui sait si l'on ne verra pas que le phosphore et l'esprit vont ensemble?" "Who knows whether it is not true that phosphorus and mind are not the same thing?" – Henri Beyle (Stendhal), Histoire de la Peinture en Italie, Chapter XCI, p. 209. (Ed. 1854)

"But, spite of all the criticising elves, Those who would make us feel, must feel themselves." – Charles Churchill, "The Rosciad" (1761), line 961.

"Below the surface stream, shallow and light, Of what we say and feel—below the stream, As light, of what we think we feel, there flows With noiseless current, strong, obscure and deep, The central stream of what we feel indeed." – Matthew Arnold, St. Paul and Protestantism (1870).
