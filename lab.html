RESEARCH DESK

Probability laboratory

Experiment with Bayesian updating and reinforcement using reproducible teaching models.

Reading lists · Research trails · Research map · Probability laboratory

Two small, fully specified teaching models. Change assumptions, inspect the numbers, and export every step. They do not reproduce or validate the linked research. Download the model implementations (JavaScript).

01 / BELIEF UPDATING

A binary Bayesian update

H is a hypothesis and E is an observation. The two hypotheses H and not-H are exhaustive. Repeated observations are assumed conditionally independent given the hypothesis, with fixed likelihoods.

P(H|E) = P(H) P(E|H) / [P(H) P(E|H) + (1 − P(H)) P(E|not-H)]

Worked example: a prior of 0.20 and likelihoods 0.80 and 0.20 give a posterior of 0.50 after E. A subsequent not-E returns it to 0.20.

02 / REINFORCEMENT

A reinforced urn

Draw one token with probability proportional to its color's current count. Replace it and add r extra tokens of that color. With r = 0 the composition stays fixed. Every run uses the displayed 32-bit seed; identical inputs reproduce identical draws.

With one token of each color, the first draw has amber probability 0.50.
